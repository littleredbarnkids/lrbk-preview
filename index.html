Made on our family farm

Hi, I'm J, a dad of two.

We're a family raising our kids on a little farm, and a lot of their learning happens right at our kitchen table. Between chores, chickens and muddy boots, we've found that the best moments come when we sit down and learn alongside them.

That's why we make open-and-go printables that invite parents, grandparents and kids to join in, not just hand over a screen. Every page is something we'd happily use with our own kids. Our goal is simple: help more families slow down and spend real, meaningful time learning together.
