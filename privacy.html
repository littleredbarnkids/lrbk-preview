Privacy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Little Red Barn Kids is a small family business. Here is what happens with your information.

Email sign-ups

When you sign up for our free printables, the sign-up form is run by our email service, Kit (kit.com). We ask only for a parent's email address and your kid's age, so we can send the right pages. Kit stores that information and sends our emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave anytime. You can read Kit's own privacy policy at kit.com.

This website

This site does not use tracking cookies, analytics or ads. Fonts and images are served from this site itself. Like any website, our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.

Other sites

Links to Etsy, Pinterest, X and Kit take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Kids

Our printables are for parents and grandparents to use with their kids. We don't collect information from children.

Contact

Questions, or want your information removed? Email hello@littleredbarnkids.com.
